About

Vishal Sriram is an MSIS graduate student at Indiana University’s Kelley School of Business, following a BS in Finance & Business Analytics, who works at the intersection of product, data, and enterprise architecture. He has designed logical/physical architectures for SharePoint/.NET and Oracle environments, modeled processes with BPMN/UML, and executed SAP S/4HANA simulations across FI/CO, MM, PP, and SD. His work spans analytics dashboards, multi-year dataset cleaning in Python/Excel, and financial modeling that ties insights to decisions. Recently he’s focused on generative-AI enablement—scoping virtual financial advisor concepts, mapping AI assistant use cases, and estimating cloud AI costs with 5-year NPV/IRR models—while leading design-thinking deliverables like opportunity solution trees, usability tests, and OKRs (e.g., the IU “Night Bus” prototype). Vishal is exploring roles in tech enablement, product management, and data-driven strategy at scale, where he can own the path from problem framing and architecture to shipped features.
